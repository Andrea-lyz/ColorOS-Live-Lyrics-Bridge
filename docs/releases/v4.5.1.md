## User-visible changes
- **Local motion-cover import** (Dynamic Artwork Provider): import a video on the manual binding page
  once the album name is filled in. Format check (video, 1 s–2 min, shorter side ≥ 320); a square SDR
  8-bit H.264 up to 1080 is copied with its original picture, anything else is centre-cropped and
  compressed to H.264 ≤ 1080, ≤ 30 fps, within 20 MB; audio dropped; stored privately by content hash,
  outside the cache; answers lock-screen requests offline.
- Manual search reads the selected storefront's Apple Music album shelf (iTunes index gaps such as US
  MAYHEM).
- Several verified same-titled editions: "Several editions" status at Strict/Standard; Loose picks
  Explicit → most songs → smallest catalog ID.
- Symbol-named albums (`=`, `÷`, `- (Deluxe)` …) bind and stay apart; matching identity refactor with
  versioned name-derived cache keys.
